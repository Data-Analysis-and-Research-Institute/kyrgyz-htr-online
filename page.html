A photo or scan of a handwritten page or a few lines. Works best with dark ink on plain paper, the page filling most of the photo and lines roughly horizontal (small tilts are corrected).

Write one or more lines, leaving a clear space between words.

Текст / Text

Click a box on the image to see the alternatives for that word.

How it works. The page is straightened, ruled lines and specks are removed, text lines are traced across the page (they may curve), and each line is split into words at the wide gaps. Every word is then read by the same recogniser as on the word page. On photographed essay pages this gives about 23 % word errors and 6 % character errors; words that touch or run into the next line are the main source of mistakes. Everything runs in your browser; the image is not uploaded.
